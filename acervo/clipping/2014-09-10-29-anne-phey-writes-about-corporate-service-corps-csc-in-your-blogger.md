Certificado of Socia Honoraria

These are my experiences in community service in Brazil and my views on the country, community, education and life.

Business Consultant, ITEVA (The Advanced Technological and Vocational Institute, Brazil)

Deployed

as IBM's top talent to Brazil's State of Fortaleza as part of IBM

Corporate Service Corp (CSC) inspired by the Peace Corp. The assignment

objectives were to collaborate with governments, NGOs and communities in

emerging markets, to help bring about meaningful and sustainable

changes, but also have developed the capabilities of emerging leaders

and built lasting relationships with clients around the world.

As business consultants, we focus covered Smarter Cities, corporate social

responsibilities, issue-based consulting, consulting in developing

markets, international leadership and team building.

The NGO of focus was ITEVA (The Advanced Technological and Vocational

Institute) in the city of Aquiraz, in the North-eastern state of Ceara,

Brazil). The project covered building a growth strategy, business plan,

customer segmentation, customer loyalty, pricing, go to market,

marketing and promotions, social media, human resource and organization

structure and information technology plan.

Cultural Exchange With Speakers from Turkey & Singapore

10th April 2014, Brazil. Published by Journalist Rgegis Oliveira, City of Acquiraz.

Department of Education Aquiraz promoted a culatural exchange with 120

students and 20 teachers with two IBM executives. Present were CEO of

ITEVA Fabio Benducci, Secretary of Education for Aquiraz Terezinha

Nehterlands, municipal secretaries for Sports and Youth Andre Luis,

Culture Rudolf Strong, Tourism Milena (deputy). The Advisor of Political

Affairs and Institutional Aquiraz, Jair Silva, said that the mayor Guimarães has

facilitated the entry of knowledge that will grow the young population

of Aquiraz and the exchange of knowledge with professionals from IBM,

has increased the vocation that many already have the right to use

technology to improve their lives. Anne

Phey, of Singapore, shared about her professional,

personal, cultural aspects. She likened technology to the Brazilian

beach surfs, where one must venture out to try. Initially one may be

swallowed by the waves as it is new, but over time, one can surf above

the waves. So it is with technology. Start with ITEVA who can empower

the teachers and students with technological resources.
